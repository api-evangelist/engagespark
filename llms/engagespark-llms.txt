# SMS, Voice, WhatsApp, and Airtime Campaigns in 200+ Countries: Survey & message hard-to-reach populations – at scale

> Engage hard-to-reach populations globally—remotely and at scale—using WhatsApp, SMS, Voice, and Airtime top ups. No coding required.

You can find engageSPARK's home page here: https://www.engagespark.com/

## Customers engageSPARK is best suited for:

Organization that need to communicate with people in low- or middle-income countries.
Available channels are SMS, automated voice calls, Meta's WhatsApp, and pre-paid airtime credits.

## Sectors engageSPARK focuses on

- NGOs and humanitarian organizations
- researchers doing research directly in low- or middle-income countries
- SMEs in low- and middle-income countries
